# Required published policies

Before production launch, legal owners must publish versioned Terms & Conditions, Privacy/POPIA Policy, Cookie Policy, Responsible Alcohol Policy, Refund Policy and Delivery Policy. Each order records the accepted policy version. The Responsible Alcohol Policy must state 18+ eligibility, valid-ID checking at delivery, refusal/return handling and no delivery to a minor or an intoxicated recipient where applicable policy/rules require refusal.
